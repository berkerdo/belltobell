I do not hear the cues

Turn up the media volume with the volume buttons while a workout runs. The cues play even when the silent switch is on.

Do the cues play when the phone is locked?

Yes. Start the workout, then lock the phone. The timer keeps running and every cue plays on time. Your music or podcast gets quieter during each cue and comes back after it.

How do I see the round on the Lock Screen?

Bell to Bell shows a Live Activity with the current round and the time left. If you do not see it, go to Settings, Bell to Bell, and turn on Live Activities.

How do I change the built-in Boxing workout?

Tap the info button next to the workout. Use Quick Setup to set the rounds, work time, rest, warm-up and cool-down, then tap Save.
