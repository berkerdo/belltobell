Privacy Policy

Bell to Bell: Round Timer

Effective October 5, 2026

The short version

Bell to Bell does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

The workouts you create are saved only on your device. They are never sent to me or to anyone else. If you delete the app, they are deleted too.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Bell to Bell saves each finished workout (type, start and end time) to Apple Health on your iPhone. It only writes workouts; it does not read any of your health data. This data stays in Apple Health on your device and is never sent to me or to anyone else. You can turn this off at any time in Settings, Health, Data Access & Devices, Bell to Bell.

Network

The app does not connect to the internet. All sounds are inside the app.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code that collects data. Apple may collect crash and usage data if you allow it in your iPhone settings; see Apple's privacy policy for details.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
